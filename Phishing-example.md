Phishing Examples

These are fake messages that students in Botswana get every day.
Read them and learn how to spot them.

Example 1: Fake Bank SMS

> "FNB ALERT: Your account is blocked. Click here to verify now: http://fnb-botswana-verify.com"

Why it is fake:
- Real banks never send you a link to unblock your account
- The link is not the real FNB website
- It tries to scare you so you click fast

What to do:
Do NOT click. Delete the message. Call your bank yourself.

Example 2: Fake Parcel SMS

> "Botswana Post: Your parcel is stuck. Pay P120 to release it: http://botspost-delivery.net"

Why it is fake:
- Botswana Post does not ask you to pay through an SMS link
- The link is fake

What to do:
Do NOT click. Go to the Post Office yourself if you are waiting for a parcel.

Example 3: Fake Scholarship

> "Congratulations! You won a P10,000 scholarship. Pay P500 to claim it on WhatsApp 7XXXXXXX"

Why it is fake:
- You did not apply for it
- Real scholarships never ask you to pay money to get money
- It uses a WhatsApp number, not a real school email

What to do:
Do NOT reply. Do NOT send money.

Example 4: Fake Lecturer on WhatsApp

> "Hi, I am your lecturer. Send me your Moodle password urgently so I can upload your marks."

Why it is fake:
- No real lecturer will ever ask for your password
- Your password is only for you

What to do:
Do NOT send your password. Tell your real lecturer in class.

Example 5: Fake Moodle Login Page

You get an email with a link to a page that looks like Moodle.
It asks for your username and password, but it steals them.

Rule to remember:
Never click a login link from an email.
Always type the Moodle website yourself in your browser.

Easy Rule For All Examples

If a message asks for:
- Your password
- Your PIN
- Your OTP code
- Or money

It is phishing. Stop. Do NOT click.
