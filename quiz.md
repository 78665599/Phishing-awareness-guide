Quick Quiz: Can You Spot Phishing?

Test yourself! Read each question and choose A, B, or C.
Answers are at the bottom. Do NOT look first!

Question 1

You get this SMS:
> "Your bank account is blocked. Click here to verify now."

What should you do?

A) Click the link fast
B) Do NOT click. Delete the message and call your bank yourself
C) Reply with your PIN to unblock it

Question 2

A stranger on WhatsApp says:
> "I am your lecturer. Send me your Moodle password."

What should you do?

A) Send your password
B) Send your password and your OTP
C) Do NOT send it. No lecturer will ever ask for your password

Question 3

You get this message:
> "You won P10,000! Pay P500 to claim your prize."

What is this?

A) A real prize
B) A phishing scam - real prizes never ask you to pay
C) A message from the school

Question 4

Which one is a sign of phishing?

A) The message says "Act now or your account will be closed!"
B) The message comes from your friend
C) The message has good spelling

Question 5

You clicked a fake link by mistake. What do you do FIRST?

A) Do nothing and hope it is okay
B) Change your password right now and tell your bank / school
C) Share the link with your friends

Question 6

A real bank will NEVER ask you for what?

A) Your name
B) Your password, PIN, or OTP code
C) Your branch

Answers

1. B - Do NOT click. Call your bank yourself.
2. C - Never share your password.
3. B - It is a scam. Do not pay.
4. A - Urgent, scary language is a red flag.
5. B - Change your password right away.
6. B - Banks never ask for password, PIN, or OTP.

Your Score

- 5 to 6 correct: Excellent! You can spot phishing!
- 3 to 4 correct: Good! Read the guide again.
- 0 to 2 correct: Please read README.md again and try again.

Stay safe. Think before you click!
