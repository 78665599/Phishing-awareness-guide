Phishing Awareness Guide

Botho University - Cybersecurity Awareness Project

About This Project
This guide helps Botho University students and staff recognise, avoid, and report phishing attacks. Phishing is one of the most common cyber threats in Botswana and around the world.

What is Phishing?
Phishing is a type of cyber attack where criminals pretend to be a trusted person or organisation, such as Botho University, a bank, or a lecturer. Their goal is to trick you into clicking a fake link, downloading malware, or sharing your personal information like your password, PIN, or OTP.

How to Spot a Phishing Message

1. Urgent or Threatening Language
Example: "Your student account will be closed today if you do not act now!"

2. Suspicious Sender Address
The name may look real, but the email is fake.
Example: Botho Support <botho-support123@gmail.com>

3. Fake Links
When you hover over the link, it goes to a different website than the real one.
Example: It says http://www.botho.ac.bw but it really goes to http://www.botho-login-secure.com

4. Requests for Sensitive Information
Botho University will NEVER ask for your password, PIN, or OTP by email or WhatsApp.

5. Poor Spelling and Grammar
Many phishing messages have spelling mistakes, strange formatting, and unprofessional language.

What To Do If You Receive a Phishing Email

1. Do NOT click any link or download any attachment.
2. Do NOT reply to the sender.
3. Take a screenshot and report it to Botho IT Support.
4. Delete the email after reporting it.
5. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, change your password immediately and inform IT Support.

How To Stay Safe Online

- Always check the sender's email address before you open a message.
- Type the website address yourself instead of clicking links in emails.
- Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for your email and student portal.
- Never share your password or OTP with anyone, even if they claim to be IT staff.
- Keep your phone and laptop software updated.
